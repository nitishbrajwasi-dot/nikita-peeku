FROM MY HEART

Meri Peeku ke naam

Meri Peeku,

Pata nahi kab tum meri zindagi ka itna khoobsurat hissa ban gayi, par aaj itna zaroor jaanta hoon ki tum mere liye bahut hi khaas ho.

Tumhe jab main Peeku kehta hoon na, toh us naam mein sirf ek nickname nahi, balki woh saari warmth hai jo tumhare liye mere dil mein hai.

Mere liye tum bahut precious ho — shayad itni ki kabhi-kabhi words bhi chhote pad jaate hain.

♡

Tum mein mujhe Radhey ki woh komalta, shanti aur prem ki jhalak dikhti hai jise dekhkar mann bas thoda aur shaant ho jaata hai.

“Tum chand nahi,
kyunki chand ko toh raat ki zarurat padti hai;
tum woh ujiyara ho,
jiske hone se raat bhi khoobsurat lagti hai.”

Main tumse kuch maangne nahi aaya, bas apne dil ki ek sachchi baat kehna chahta hoon —

I love you, Peeku. ❤️

Ye kehna mere liye koi filmy dialogue nahi hai. Ye bas ek simple sa confession hai ki tum mere liye ordinary nahi ho.

Tumhari smile, tumhari baatein, tumhara simple sa hona — ye sab mere liye bahut precious hai.

Aur agar prem ka matlab sirf kisi ko paana nahi, balki uski khushi, respect aur sukoon ki dil se care karna hai, toh haan…

main tumse prem karta hoon.

Bas itna hi kehna tha, Peeku — tum meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat feelings mein se ek ho.

— tumhara, dil se
